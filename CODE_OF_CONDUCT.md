# Code of conduct

## Commitment

Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, or any other project space — should be able to do so without
harassment, whatever their background, identity, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept technical criticism constructively, with evidence where you can.
- Keep reports and examples free of other people's private data. The same rule
  that keeps prompts and credentials out of this repository applies to people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or any conduct that would reasonably be
  considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the contact listed on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sitr3n01). Reports are kept
confidential. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that break this code, and may restrict or ban participants who do
so — temporarily or permanently, according to the severity and repetition of the
behavior.
